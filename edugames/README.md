# Truck & Dino School

A learning game for a 4-year-old, built as a single self-contained `index.html` (no installs, no build step). Every prompt is read aloud, so no reading is needed.

## Games
- **Numbers** — monster-truck missions: jump the gap, stop the runaway truck, boost (counting, "one more", addition)
- **Colors** — draw a road to drive the car through numbered color checkpoints
- **Shapes** — move the quarterback with ← → and press SPACE to throw through the right shape, dodging defenders
- **Trace** — trace dinos, vehicles and animals; Big level types and traces your name
- **Dino Jump** — endless runner with a jump counter and best score
- **Parking Lot** — slide cars to free the red car (5×5 Little, 8×8 Big), with a help button
- **Blocks** — stack bricks with gravity and tipping physics; Little fills in see-through shadows, Big copies a picture or free-builds
- **Map Maker** — sand-table island builder: mountains, water, volcanoes, roads and trucks, rain, day/night, treasure hunt
- **Beats** — drum machine with Rock, Robot, Silly, Truck and Dino kits; Little is 4×8 always on the beat, Big is 6×16 with loud hits, drum rolls, bouncy swing, sound swapping and quantized live recording
- **Brick Smash** — breakout with a monster-truck paddle (finger or ← →); Little has 10 balls at one slow speed, Big has 5 balls, bigger walls, tough bricks and a ball that speeds up
- **Paint** — paint by number with 8 pictures; Little is a chunky grid with 4 colors, Big is a finer grid with 6–10 colors and more detail
- **Sky Flyer** — tap-to-fly biplane through air-race towers with a best score; Big adds a fuel gauge and gas cans to collect

Little kid / Big kid buttons on the menu set the difficulty.

## Play
Online at https://chasealondon.com/edugames/ (not linked from the site and marked noindex), or open `index.html` in a browser. For a locked-down kid mode on Windows, make a shortcut:

```
"C:\Program Files (x86)\Microsoft\Edge\Application\msedge.exe" --kiosk "file:///C:/path/to/index.html" --edge-kiosk-type=fullscreen
```

Edge gives the most natural-sounding voices. Grown-ups exit a game by holding the 🏠 button for 2 seconds; Alt+F4 closes kiosk mode.
