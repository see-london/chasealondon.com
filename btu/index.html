How this works
  • Sizing: a points model. Home size sets the base load, and each answer adds or removes points, the same way my original version weighted factors learned from real housing data with known system sizes. This rebuild uses published rules of thumb, not that data.
  • Climate: your ZIP sets a climate band (hot, mixed or cold) that adjusts the size and the hours your AC runs each year.
  • Prices: installed costs are mid-range 2026 estimates for a 3-ton system, scaled by size. Outdoor unit only is estimated at 70% of a full system. Maintenance is $150 a year for a new unit. An older unit's efficiency drops about 1.5% a year while its maintenance and repair costs climb.
  • This is an estimate for planning, not a quote. It's a personal project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any manufacturer.
